# Changelog

## Version 1.3.0

- Every part of Keyhole now has its own music: "Lobby Lights" on the menu, "After Hours" for the daily vault, "Velvet Tumblers" for practice, the calm "Still Water" for Zen vaults and the bright "Open House" for Theme vaults.
- Music fades smoothly from one song to the next as you move around, and each song loops while you play.

## Version 1.2.0

- New: Zen Vault. No guess limit and no way to fail. Every lock opens eventually, and your score is how many guesses it took. Beat your personal best.
- New: Theme Vaults. Pick a topic (Animals, Food, Nature, Halloween, Space, Music, or Sports & Games) and every word in the vault fits it.
- Stats now include Zen and Theme vaults, and How to Play explains the new modes.

## Version 1.1.0

- Android: Keyhole now checks GitHub for new versions. When one is out, tap Update now and Android asks you to confirm the install. Your stats, streak and gold keys are kept. You can turn the check off, or check by hand, in Options.
- The first time you update, Android asks you to allow Keyhole to install apps. That's needed for the built-in updater.

## Version 1.0.0

First release of Keyhole, the daily word vault.

- A new vault every day: three locks (a 4-, 5- and 6-letter word) and 15 guesses shared between them.
- Every guess turns the tumblers: letters Click (right spot), Wiggle (wrong spot) or stay Silent.
- Pins: an opened lock's letters carry over. Test them for free on the next lock to learn where its letters are. Open a lock in 3 guesses or fewer for a second test.
- Gold keys for opening the daily vault with 6 or more guesses to spare, and six lock styles to unlock with them.
- Streaks, statistics and a shareable result.
- Unlimited practice vaults.
- Original music and mechanical sound effects, high-contrast tiles, fullscreen and a built-in updater.
