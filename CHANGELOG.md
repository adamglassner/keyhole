# Changelog

## Version 1.0.0

First release of Keyhole, the daily word vault.

- A new vault every day: three locks (a 4-, 5- and 6-letter word) and 15 guesses shared between them.
- Every guess turns the tumblers: letters Click (right spot), Wiggle (wrong spot) or stay Silent.
- Pins: an opened lock's letters carry over. Test them for free on the next lock to learn where its letters are. Open a lock in 3 guesses or fewer for a second test.
- Gold keys for opening the daily vault with 6 or more guesses to spare, and six lock styles to unlock with them.
- Streaks, statistics and a shareable result.
- Unlimited practice vaults.
- Original music and mechanical sound effects, high-contrast tiles, fullscreen and a built-in updater.
